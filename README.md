# Aurelia

A tablet register for dinner service — plated dishes on the left, the live guest check on the right.

![Aurelia register during night service: photo menu, Table 3 guest check, and Charge](https://here.now/og/liminal-thistle-yp2j.jpg)

## Who it's for

Servers and floor leads on a 10–12" landscape tablet. You stay on the pass: ring the table, fire the kitchen, glance at the room — without hunting through a back-office screen.

## What you can do

- Build a check from the photo menu — tap to add, then set doneness, sides, and notes
- Split, hold, or charge (cash, card, or Apple Pay) and drop a printed receipt
- Advance kitchen tickets from pending → preparing → ready
- Read tonight's take, table turns, and the room map (vacant, occupied, reserved, billed)
- Tap an occupied table to pull its ticket straight back onto the register

## Try it

[Open the live demo](https://liminal-thistle-yp2j.here.now/) — landscape feels like the real tablet. Nothing is charged. Reset with the rotate icon on the rail.

Run it on your machine in [docs/SETUP.md](docs/SETUP.md).

## How it works

It's a frontend demo: menu, tickets, kitchen, and sales live in memory. There is no backend, and nothing is billed. Night mode is dinner service; the sun icon on the rail switches to linen day service. One live ticket feeds the register and the floor board.
